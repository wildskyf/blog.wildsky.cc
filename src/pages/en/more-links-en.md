## Places I've Been/Am Currently At
Whether past or present, places that have accepted me and given me a sense of belonging deserve to be included here.

### Mozilla Taiwan Community
A veteran free software community in Taiwan, an excellent environment for information technology students, with many projects to contribute to and enhance project participation skills, and many enthusiastic seniors willing to mentor newcomers.
- [https://moztw.org](https://moztw.org)

### womany
A website focused on gender-related information, covering all aspects of women's issues, from career development, travel and entertainment, fashion and makeup, lifestyle to love and sexuality. It's a website with profound educational significance.
- [https://womany.net](https://womany.net/)

### Taiwan AI Labs
A privately funded research organization in Taipei, focusing on healthcare, smart cities, and human-computer interaction.
- [https://ailabs.tw](https://ailabs.tw)
